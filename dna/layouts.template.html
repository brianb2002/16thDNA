THE THREE MODES (SAME IN EVERY LAYOUT)

DASHBOARD

The hologram office. Look around from the chair, use the buttons on the desk, and see the coach, clocks and lessons around you.

FULL SCREEN

The room goes away. Chart, ticket, coach and watchlist fill the screen as a clean 2D layout for focused trading on a laptop or phone.

SIMULATION

Pick a platform (Coinbase, TradingView, Webull, NinjaTrader, thinkorswim, MetaTrader). The center screen becomes a practice replica of its layout, so you learn where everything is before trading there for real. The coach still explains each step.

DEV MODE

The DEV button stays in every layout and mode, so you can still move, add, recolor or erase anything yourself.
